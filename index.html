Research Interests

My research focuses on Empirical Asset Pricing and Financial Engineering, with an emphasis on using Machine Learning to discover market anomalies and optimize factor construction. I am also interested in Climate Finance, Climate Policy, and the interdisciplinary application of AI in finance, particularly in quantifying climate risks, analyzing textual disclosures, and their impacts on asset valuations in emerging markets.
