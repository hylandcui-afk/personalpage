Research Interests

My research focuses on Empirical Asset Pricing, Corporate Finance, and Climate Finance in emerging markets. Methodologically, I specialize in applying natural language processing, deep learning (GNNs, Transformers, LLMs), and causal inference to large-scale financial and textual datasets, with a focus on constructing novel firm-level measures and testing their asset-pricing implications.
